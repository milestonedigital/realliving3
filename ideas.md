# REALiving Website Redesign — Design Ideas

## Context
REALiving® is a premium Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provider based in Chippewa Falls, WI.
Their brand is centered around authenticity, growth, and helping people "Be Their BEST®."
The color palette is teal/turquoise with dark navy accents.

## Idea 1: "Organic Modernism" — Warm Teal Editorial

**Design Movement:** Organic Modernism meets Editorial Wellness

**Core Principles:**
1. Asymmetric editorial layouts with intentional white space as a breathing room
2. Humanistic warmth — photography-forward with large, full-bleed imagery
3. Typographic hierarchy that feels editorial (magazine-like), not corporate
4. Teal as a living, breathing color — not flat, but with depth through gradients and overlays

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Deep Teal `#1A7A70` — authority and trust
- Secondary: Warm Teal `#3BBFB5` — approachable and fresh
- Accent: Warm Cream `#F8F4EE` — warmth and authenticity
- Dark: Deep Forest `#0D3B35` — grounding and premium
- Text: Charcoal `#2A2A2A` — readable and human

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Offset grid with content bleeding into margins
- Hero section: full-viewport with diagonal text placement
- Services: staggered card layout with alternating image/text
- Navigation: sticky top bar with subtle blur backdrop

**Signature Elements:**
1. Organic blob shapes as section dividers (SVG)
2. Large typographic pull-quotes in script font
3. Teal gradient overlays on photography

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth scroll with parallax on hero
- Cards lift on hover with shadow depth
- Navigation items with underline slide animation

**Animation:**
- Fade-up entrance animations on scroll (Framer Motion)
- Hero text stagger animation
- Subtle parallax on background images

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Playfair Display` — elegant, editorial, trustworthy
- Body: `DM Sans` — modern, readable, approachable
- Accent: `Dancing Script` — for script headings (matching brand)

## Idea 2: "Structured Clarity" — Premium Corporate Wellness

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Style meets Contemporary Wellness

**Core Principles:**
1. Grid-based precision with intentional asymmetry in hero sections
2. Bold typographic statements as visual anchors
3. Generous negative space as a luxury signal
4. Color used sparingly but with maximum impact

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Teal `#2BA99E` — fresh, modern, trustworthy
- Dark: Deep Teal `#0E4D47` — premium and authoritative
- Light: Soft Mint `#E8F7F5` — clean section backgrounds
- Neutral: Warm White `#FAFAF8` — main background
- Accent: Gold `#C9A84C` — premium highlight moments

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Strict 12-column grid with deliberate breaks
- Full-width hero with split content (text left, visual right)
- Services in a clean 3-column card grid
- Strong horizontal rules as structural elements

**Signature Elements:**
1. Large numbered sections (01, 02, 03) as visual anchors
2. Thin gold accent lines on key headings
3. Bold uppercase section labels in small caps

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Precise, controlled hover states
- Clean slide-in animations from left
- Minimal but purposeful micro-interactions

**Animation:**
- Counter animations for statistics
- Smooth page transitions
- Subtle border animations on CTA buttons

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Cormorant Garamond` — refined and prestigious
- Body: `Nunito Sans` — clean and professional
- Labels: `Space Grotesk` — technical precision

## Idea 3: "Living Canvas" — Immersive Wellness Experience

**Design Movement:** Biophilic Design meets Digital Wellness

**Core Principles:**
1. Nature-inspired textures and organic shapes throughout
2. Immersive full-screen sections that tell a story
3. Typography as visual art — large, expressive, intentional
4. Movement and life in every scroll interaction

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Ocean Teal `#2AADA3` — vitality and clarity
- Deep: Forest Night `#0B3D38` — depth and wisdom
- Warm: Sand `#F5EFE6` — natural warmth
- Fresh: Sage `#7BAE9C` — growth and renewal
- Pure: White `#FFFFFF` — clarity and space

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Fullscreen sections with scroll-triggered reveals
- Overlapping layers creating depth
- Diagonal section breaks with SVG wave dividers
- Content floating over background imagery

**Signature Elements:**
1. Animated SVG wave dividers between sections
2. Floating botanical/geometric decorative elements
3. Overlapping text and image compositions

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Scroll-triggered animations that feel like breathing
- Hover states that reveal hidden content layers
- Smooth morphing shapes on interaction

**Animation:**
- Parallax scrolling on hero and section backgrounds
- Text reveal animations (mask reveal)
- Floating particle effects in hero

**Typography System:**
- Display: `Fraunces` — organic, expressive, unique
- Body: `Source Sans 3` — clean and readable
- Accent: `Caveat` — handwritten authenticity

## Selected Design: Idea 1 — "Organic Modernism"

**Rationale:** This approach best matches REALiving's brand identity — warm, authentic, editorial, and professional. The combination of Playfair Display (editorial authority) with DM Sans (modern readability) creates a premium feel without being cold or corporate. The teal palette is preserved and elevated with depth and warmth.
